# Stats

The built-in `/stats` page, no external dashboard needed:

- **Core numbers** — total watch time, episode count, completion rate, mean score,
  score distribution.
- **Top genres and studios** — among your completed shows.
- **Watch-activity heatmap** — a calendar view of when you actually watch, at a glance.
- **Year in anime** — an annual wrap-up: what you watched, your standout shows, and
  how the year compares to your overall habits.
- **Drop-pattern breakdown** — the genres, tags, and even common words in titles that
  show up most often in what you drop, drawn from your [drop reasons](personal-notes.md)
  and library data. Useful for spotting a pattern you didn't consciously notice ("I
  drop almost everything tagged 'slow burn' after 6 episodes").
- **Rewatch data** — a most-rewatched list and distribution, drawn from repeat counts
  and rewatch notes.
- **Recommendation outcomes** — a small card showing the recommender's real hit rate
  (recommendations that turned into something you actually added or rated well) and a
  breakdown of why you've dismissed past recommendations.
- **Streaming coverage summary** — a compact cross-link into the full
  [Streaming coverage](streaming-coverage.md) page.

## Personal library health

Settings → Sync also has a smaller "Library health" card — a personal, always-visible
view of the same data-quality signals admins see instance-wide (orphaned notes, stale
recommendations, sync drift, recent sync failures), scoped to just your own account. If
everything's clean it just says so; if not, it lists specifics with links to the
affected anime.
